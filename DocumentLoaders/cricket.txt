Under the sun, the players stand,
With bat and ball held close at hand.
The bowler runs, the crowd goes still,
A thunderous cheer awaits the thrill.

The bat swings hard, the ball takes flight,
A six disappears beyond our sight.
The wickets fall, the pressure grows,
Yet every player fights till the close.

From dusty fields to stadium lights,
Cricket brings dreams to endless heights.
Win or lose, through joy and pain,
The love for cricket will still remain.

For every run and every cheer,
For every victory we hold dear,
Cricket is more than just a game—
It lives in hearts and lights the flame.
